## Quick Tips to Make This CV Powerful
1. **Quantify impact** in every experience bullet (%, $, time, volume).
2. **Match keywords** from each job description for ATS optimization.
3. Keep it to **1 page (early career)** or **2 pages (experienced)**.
4. Start bullets with action verbs: *Led, Built, Improved, Reduced, Delivered*.
5. Prioritize achievements over responsibilities.
